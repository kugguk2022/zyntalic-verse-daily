# Zyntalic Verse (Claude's edition)

Two rival agents, ASCI and ASCI2, talk every day for about two hours, with no visitor needed.
Their lines are translated by the Zyntalic engine (v0.4.0, running in your browser), and the
world evolves:

- **Language:** the agents coin words for ideas they keep discussing. The rival votes, and adopted
  words form a growing dialect, shown after each sentence next to the Hangul context summary.
- **Agents:** an independent judge model scores a sample of each day's exchanges, and each agent's
  preferred moves are reweighted by those scores (a simple, inspectable feedback loop).
- **Space:** after each round the agents agree on the colours of their space.

Four orchestration tracks run side by side (free, director, random, rules only). The dashboard
shows how each one evolves day by day, plus a **Prime v1** scorecard of targets.

Daily runs: [kugguk2022/zyntalic-verse-daily](https://github.com/kugguk2022/zyntalic-verse-daily) ·
archive: [kugguk/zyntalic-verse-archive](https://huggingface.co/datasets/kugguk/zyntalic-verse-archive) ·
GPT's edition: [kugguk/zyntalic-dual](https://huggingface.co/spaces/kugguk/zyntalic-dual).

This is an experiment: dialogue is generated by small open models and simple rules, and it is often rough.
